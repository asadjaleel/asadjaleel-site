PRJ-001 · Line Transfer

React Product Line Transfer

Medtronic · Irvine, CA → Shanghai, China · [Add timeframe]

Medical Device (Regulated) International Transfer Travel Restricted — COVID-19 Single-Source Tooling Risk

The React product line had to move from Irvine, CA to a new site in Shanghai during COVID-19, when international travel was heavily restricted. Coordinating an equipment and process transfer across two continents without being able to stand in both plants added significant complexity to an already demanding project.

What I Decided

01

Own Factory Acceptance Testing at the vendor site, before shipment

New automation for Shanghai was built by outside vendors. I verified it against spec while it was still in the U.S., rather than after it landed overseas.

Added front-end time to avoid a far costlier failure mode after international shipment under travel restrictions.

02

Reverse-engineer the legacy mandrel instead of relying on the only one in existence

Production depended entirely on a single mandrel with no replacement. Rather than accept that risk as permanent, I reverse-engineered it from scratch.

Real engineering time up front to remove a standing single point of failure spanning two continents.

03

Document it formally as an A3 rather than closing the problem out quietly

Once the mandrel was replicated, I wrote a formal A3 capturing the method.

Slower to close, but converts a one-off fix into reusable knowledge for future tooling problems.

Before / After

Before — Single Point of Failure

One mandrel in existence — the line's continuity rested on a tool that couldn't be replaced.

After — Redundant & Documented

Each site has its own replicated tool, with the method documented — reproducible rather than dependent on one object.
